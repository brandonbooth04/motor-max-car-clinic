About Us

Recognised Excellence in Automotive Care

Motor Max Car Clinic is proud to be a MIWA 5-Star Graded Workshop – one of the highest industry recognitions for quality, safety, and professionalism in South Africa.

We are also officially RMI (Retail Motor Industry Organisation) Registered, giving our clients the assurance that every repair meets strict industry standards.

Our team of skilled technicians brings years of hands-on experience to every job, treating each vehicle with the care it deserves. From your first visit, you'll see why our loyal clients keep coming back.
