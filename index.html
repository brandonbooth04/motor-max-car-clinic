Who We Are

Precision Care.

Motor Max Car Clinic is a MIWA 5-Star Graded Workshop — one of the highest industry recognitions for quality, safety, and professionalism in South Africa. We are also officially RMI Registered, ensuring every repair meets strict industry standards.

Our skilled technicians bring years of hands-on experience across all makes and models. We specialise in Mercedes-Benz automatic gearbox repairs alongside the full range of automotive services.
